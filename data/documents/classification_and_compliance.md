# Risk Classification and Compliance Disclaimers

## Phase 3 Classification Boundaries
The anomaly score generated by the 5-rule engine is categorized into four operational tiers:
- **GREEN (Score 0–19)**: Low empirical divergence across evaluated scoring rules. Requires complete data sufficiency (`score_completeness == "COMPLETE"`).
- **AMBER (Score 20–49)**: Moderate divergence, typically triggered by a single elevated risk rule (such as an isolated mirror trade discrepancy).
- **RED (Score 50–100)**: Substantial multi-rule divergence, indicating cumulative anomalies across pricing, volumes, and bilateral declarations.
- **INSUFFICIENT_DATA**: Assigned whenever score completeness is `PARTIAL` or `UNAVAILABLE`.

## Data Sufficiency Guardrails
- **No False Greens**: A transaction is **never** classified as `GREEN` when required risk rules could not be evaluated due to missing source data.
- **Flag Status Independence**: Anomaly flagging (`FLAGGED` vs `NOT_FLAGGED`) is tracked separately from color categories, ensuring complete transparency into whether any individual rule exceeded its threshold.

## Legal and Regulatory Disclaimers
Risk anomaly scores, color tiers, and AI explanations generated by MineTrace represent statistical divergence indicators and supply-chain monitoring heuristics. They do not constitute formal legal findings of fraud, criminal misconduct, or export control violations.
